## Overview

Official web platform for **SKS Trinity Infrabuild**, civil engineering contractors, structural retrofitting experts, and waterproofing specialists based in Gomti Nagar, Lucknow. The website features an executive **Mobile-First Light Theme** with an architectural **Logoholic Background Atmosphere** aligned with the official corporate logo color palette and typography:

- **Mobile-First Architecture:** Responsive fluid containers, touch-optimized targets (>=48px), sticky mobile quick-action conversion bar (Call, WhatsApp, Consult), and zero horizontal overflow.
- **Logoholic Architectural Atmosphere:** High-resolution architectural watermarks of the official SKS Trinity Infrabuild superstructure emblem embedded subtly (opacity 0.035 - 0.055) into section canvases (Hero, About, Services, Methodology, Quality, Leadership, Contact, and Footer) with a blueprint grid background.
- **Direct Email Dispatch to `skstrinityinfrabuild@gmail.com`:** Real-time client consultation requests emailed straight to `skstrinityinfrabuild@gmail.com` via FormSubmit AJAX with loading spinners, instant confirmation, and native form POST fallback.
- **Primary Headings Typography:** **`Outfit`** (Google Fonts) — sharp, geometric, modern architectural cuts.
- **Body & Content Typography:** **`Plus Jakarta Sans`** (Google Fonts) — open counters, exceptional readability and hierarchy.
- **Logo Charcoal Slate:** `#1E2832` / `#141C24` (Top bar, footers, headers, crisp high-contrast typography)
- **Logo Metallic Gold:** `#C5A038` / `#9A7B1A` (Primary brand accent, icons, badges, borders)
- **Logo Gomti Wave Blue:** `#2B5876` / `#4A7C99` (Secondary brand accent, retrofitting badges)
- **Specialized Waterproofing Teal:** `#0D9488` (Advanced chemical & membrane waterproofing accent)
- **Architectural Canvas:** `#F8F9FA` with pure `#FFFFFF` card surfaces
